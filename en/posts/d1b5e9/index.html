Why automate operations?

Running Facebook campaigns and maintaining an online store take up a significant share of an ecommerce operator’s time. Automation tools such as n8n can help with:

  1. Data collection: checking competitors’ prices on a schedule.
  2. Notifications: sending order-status updates to your phone.
  3. Campaign operations: producing creative assets in batches and monitoring returns.

Facebook Live Commerce — From Account Setup to Campaign Operations

Created2026-04-02|Updated2026-04-02|Global Growth
|Post Views:
English edition, translated and edited from the Chinese article. Figures and product details refer to the original publication date. 中文原文

TL;DR: The original article organizes Facebook live commerce around targeted promotion, professional product demonstrations and products suited to the market. It covers account preparation, a five-step campaign workflow and two conversion approaches.

What makes a live-commerce session work?

The article argues that product quality, supply-chain credibility and the opportunity to speak to a real person matter especially for jade, jewelry and other expensive products.

Its operating model is:

Targeted promotion + professional demonstration + market-specific product selection → orders.

1. Prepare the operating tools

Page-management accounts

The original setup assigns streaming, customer support and backup responsibilities to two or three page administrators. Personal accounts manage the business page; the page is the public destination.

A dedicated business page

Communicate the offer clearly:

  • A focused position, such as factory-direct jade or premium wholesale supply.
  • The product category, markets served and supply advantages.
  • A contact route through WhatsApp or the relevant business messaging service.

An advertising account

The original workflow calls for a business advertising account connected to Business Manager and the page, with a stable network connection.

2. Set up the accounts

Build the page

  1. Open page management.
  2. Set the name, category and business description.
  3. Add a call-to-action button for customer contact.
  4. Assign the people responsible for streaming and support.

Configure page management

The original checklist covers posting permissions, geographic restrictions and comment filters.

Connect the advertising account

Link the advertising account to the page, check the connection and test the campaign workflow before the live session.

3. Promote the live session

Two conversion approaches

Approach in the original workflow Reach Inquiry quality Intended use
Ad-driven page traffic Higher More varied Initial reach and viewership
Messaging applications Lower More qualified Conversations and conversion

The article gives a 70/30 allocation as an example of combining the approaches.

Five-step workflow

  1. Create a campaign. Use a name such as “Live demo — product category.” The original article suggests testing an engagement objective before scaling.
  2. Configure the ad set. The described workflow selects the ad destination and a goal of maximizing Facebook page visits.
  3. Choose geography and language. Match the product to the intended market. The jade example includes Southeast Asia, Hong Kong, Taiwan and overseas Chinese audiences, with appropriate Chinese and English creative.
  4. Define the audience. The article starts with relevant audience interests and moves to customer-based custom and lookalike audiences after conversions accumulate. Its age-range examples are 28–65 for jade and 20–45 for apparel.
  5. Select creative and publish. Use an existing preview post, prepare the reply that directs people to the live session, then publish the campaign.

4. Adjust the budget by stage

Rehearsal stage

The original article suggests $50–100 per day as an example budget for practicing the process and presentation, without expecting immediate conversion.

Established operation

  • Start warming up the page three to five days before a session.
  • Combine direct promotion and messaging on the day.
  • Increase spending according to conversion results.

Monitor views, click-through rate, viewing duration and cost during the stream. Pause or adjust ad sets that perform poorly.

5. Produce differentiated creative

The proposed structure is:

Clear product footage + knowledgeable presentation + a specific offer.

An example preview shows a presenter explaining a jade bangle’s material and workmanship, with the session time, a limited offer and shipping information.

The original article also suggests factory and inventory footage, customer feedback with personal details obscured, and discussion of the product’s collection value.

Operating checklist

  • Assign page managers and support responsibilities.
  • Prepare the business page.
  • Configure the advertising account.
  • Test network stability.
  • Set permissions and moderation controls.
  • Publish a preview post.
  • Configure the live-session campaign.
  • Monitor campaign and session data while streaming.

The core lesson is that a live session needs coordinated promotion, presentation and customer follow-up.

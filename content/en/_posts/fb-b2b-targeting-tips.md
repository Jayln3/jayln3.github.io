> **TL;DR:** A common B2B advertising problem is paying for inquiries from individual shoppers. The original article proposes three filters: B2B audience criteria, visible minimum-order quantities, and job-title targeting.

## The problem: consumer traffic absorbs the budget

Cross-border suppliers may receive plenty of inquiries from people who want one item or are simply comparing prices, while reaching few people with purchasing authority.

The original article argues that broad consumer-oriented audiences need additional qualification for B2B campaigns. The following techniques are its proposed filters.

## 1. Combine interests with B2B criteria

### Setup

In the ad set's detailed-targeting controls, the original workflow uses a “must also match” field:

1. Add industry interests, such as jade wholesale or jewelry supply chains.
2. Add qualifying terms such as wholesale, supply-chain management, corporate purchasing and B2B.

The intended logic is to reach people who show both industry interest and a business-related attribute.

### Results reported in the original article

| Audience setup | Consumer-traffic share | Cost per B2B inquiry |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Interests only | About 65% | $28–35 |
| Interests plus additional criteria | About 15% | $12–18 |

The original article attributes these figures to factory campaigns and notes that results vary by category.

## 2. Put minimum-order quantities in the copy

State the wholesale threshold directly:

> “Jade bangles: minimum order of 50 pieces, with private-label customization.”
>
> “Factory-direct apparel: minimum order of 500 pieces, with cross-border logistics.”

An individual shopper can immediately see whether the offer fits. A wholesale buyer sees a signal that the seller can handle supply and production.

This can also improve qualification: a buyer who submits an inquiry after reading the threshold already understands a key commercial condition.

## 3. Focus on purchasing decision-makers

This approach becomes more useful once a campaign has a base of converted customers:

1. Upload the customer list to create a custom audience.
2. Build a lookalike audience from it.
3. In the targeting workflow described by the original article, add relevant job titles:
   - Purchasing Manager
   - Production Manager
   - CEO, Founder or Owner
   - Supply Chain Manager

Interest alone does not establish purchasing authority. The purpose of the job-title layer is to focus on people involved in purchase decisions.

## Combine the three techniques

The proposed conversion path is:

1. **Ad set**: industry interests, qualifying B2B criteria and job roles.
2. **Creative**: minimum-order quantity, factory capabilities and an offer for business buyers.
3. **Destination**: the business page, WhatsApp replies and a quotation guide.

| Technique | Purpose | Difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Additional audience criteria | Qualify incoming traffic | Low |
| Minimum-order copy | Qualify buyers and communicate the offer | Low |
| Job-title targeting | Reach decision-makers | Moderate |

The central idea is to combine audience selection with clear commercial information. The original article describes examples from jade, jewelry, apparel and consumer-electronics suppliers.
